# Business Problem Overview

Many growing restaurants still rely on manual records or disconnected systems to manage their daily operations. As the business grows, these methods become difficult to manage and reduce overall efficiency.

---

# Identified Problems

## 1. Slow Billing Process

Manual or outdated billing methods increase customer waiting time, especially during busy hours.

---

## 2. Limited Sales Visibility

Restaurant owners often do not have clear daily, weekly, or monthly sales reports, making business decisions difficult.

---

## 3. Difficult Profit Tracking

Without proper reporting, it is hard to understand actual profit, expenses, and overall business performance.

---

## 4. Customer Record Management

Customer details, order history, and repeat customer information are not managed in a structured way.

---

## 5. Inventory Monitoring

Basic stock tracking is often done manually, leading to inaccurate inventory information and unnecessary wastage.

---

## 6. Multiple Separate Processes

Billing, reporting, customer management, and inventory are handled using different methods instead of one integrated system.

---

# Business Impact

These problems result in:

- Increased operational time
- Higher chance of human error
- Poor decision making
- Reduced customer satisfaction
- Difficulty in business growth

---

# Problem Statement

Growing restaurants need a simple, affordable, and centralized system that helps manage their daily operations efficiently without increasing complexity.

---

# Success After Solving These Problems

After implementing Saru POS, restaurant owners should be able to:

- Complete billing faster.
- View accurate sales reports.
- Track business performance.
- Manage customer information.
- Monitor inventory more effectively.
